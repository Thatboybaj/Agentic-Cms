This workflow lets one person produce 4–6 SEO-optimized, AEO-ready blog posts per week using Claude + cheap tooling. The human's job is direction, quality gate, and publishing. Claude does the heavy lifting.

1

Keyword mining (30 min/week — batch)

Use Ahrefs Lite ($29/mo) or Semrush free tier to pull 20–30 target keywords per week. Prioritize: keyword difficulty under 30, question-format keywords ("how to", "what is", "best way to"), and competitor gap keywords. Drop into a shared Notion or Airtable tracker with assigned intent type and target pillar.

Ahrefs Lite / SemrushAirtable trackerBatch Monday AM
2

SERP research prompt → Claude

For each keyword, run a structured prompt in Claude that produces: (a) a content brief with H1, H2/H3 structure, target entities, related keywords, and FAQ section, (b) an AEO answer block in 40–60 words for direct answer formatting, (c) a meta title + description. Store the brief in Notion. This takes ~5 mins per keyword with a saved prompt template.

Claude brief generatorSaved prompt templatesNotion briefs
Example prompt: "You are an SEO strategist for Grandeur, a marketing OS. Write a content brief for the keyword: [keyword]. Include: H1, H2/H3 outline, 5 related entities, 8 LSI keywords, a 50-word AEO answer block, meta title, meta description, and 3 internal linking opportunities."
3

Full post generation in Claude

Pass the brief back to Claude with your BrandPack context (Grandeur's tone, do/don'ts, positioning) and request the full 1,500–2,500 word article. Use separate Claude turns for each major section to maintain quality. Always include: intro with the keyword in the first 100 words, FAQ section at the bottom (minimum 4 questions), one CTA block pointing to a relevant Grandeur feature.

Claude ProjectsBrand voice system promptSection-by-section
4

Quality gate + keyword optimization (human, 15 min)

Human reviews for: factual accuracy, brand voice, keyword density (1–2% target), heading structure, FAQ quality, internal links, and CTA relevance. Use Surfer SEO ($49/mo) or Frase ($45/mo) content editor for keyword coverage scoring before publishing. This is the only mandatory human step.

Surfer SEO / FraseHuman quality gate15 min per post
5

Publish + technical SEO layer

Publish via Ghost or WordPress. Auto-generate: schema markup (Article + FAQ schema using RankMath or Yoast), internal links using a link-building SOP, canonical tags, and Open Graph tags for social sharing. Use Make.com (Integromat) to automatically post a social teaser to LinkedIn + X on publish. Total publish time: 10–15 min.

Ghost / WordPressRankMath / YoastMake.com automationFAQ schema
6

Monthly update cycle (compound returns)

Every month, use GSC data to identify posts ranking on page 2 (positions 11–20). These are your highest-leverage update targets. Re-run Claude with: "Update this post, add more depth to sections X and Y, add 2 more FAQs, and refresh the statistics." Publishing an update triggers a re-crawl and often a ranking jump within 2–4 weeks.

AEO format rules

Every post must include:

  • A 40–60 word direct answer immediately below the H1
  • FAQ section at bottom with minimum 4 questions (FAQ schema)
  • Definition blocks for any jargon: "What is a BrandPack?"
  • Numbered lists for processes ("5 steps to...")
  • Comparison tables (Grandeur vs alternatives)
  • HowTo schema where relevant

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) angle: To get Grandeur cited by ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — create content that gets backlinked by other authoritative sites. The more external sites reference your definitions of "Marketing OS" or "BrandPack workflow", the more likely AI engines absorb your framing as the canonical answer. Write posts so good that people quote them.
