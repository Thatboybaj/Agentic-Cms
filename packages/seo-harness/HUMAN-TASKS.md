# What Still Needs the Human Content Marketing Specialist

The agents cover research, drafting, auditing, monitoring, and prospecting. These responsibilities cannot be delegated to them, either because agents lack the access, the identity, or the judgment context.

> CMS v0.2 change (founder direction C10–C13, 27 Sep 2026): with a site's autopost on (the default), two former human tasks move to agents: the per-post quality gate (section 2) and producing the images from the production sheets (section 4). The rest is unchanged. What the human newly owns is **approving ideas** before they become briefs.

## 1. One-time setup and credentials (a few hours, once per site)
- Create and verify Google Search Console, GA4, Bing Webmaster accounts (logins, 2FA, DNS verification)
- Connect CMS, hosting, SSL; approve any DNS or infrastructure changes the technical monitor recommends
- Set up the tool stack accounts and billing (Ahrefs/Semrush, Surfer/Frase, Make.com, Canva)
- Connect the image providers the visual-asset-agent will use (image MCP servers, image APIs, licensed web image sources) and set the monthly image cap
- Decide the site's autopost setting (on by default) and which image slots, if any, are human-only
- Approve the SEO action plan itself: pillars, budget, cadence are business decisions the agent only proposes

## 2. Idea approval, and the quality gate only when autopost is off
- **Approve ideas** (every site, always): an idea becomes a brief only after the specialist or an admin approves it
- With autopost on: no per-post task. The CMS agent resolves every [VERIFY] marker (sources the claim or rewrites without it), accepts soft blocks with a logged reason, and merges on green CI. Posts it cannot clear land in In review with a ledger item; handle those
- With autopost off (about 15 min per post, mandatory):
  - Factual accuracy check on every draft: resolve all [VERIFY] markers before publishing
  - Brand voice sign-off: does this sound like us, would we stand behind it publicly
  - Final publish click and CMS scheduling

## 3. Information gain (the ranking moat, ongoing)
- Supply the original ingredient per post the agent flags as missing: first-hand experience, client anecdotes (anonymized and permissioned), proprietary data, contrarian takes you can defend
- Run or commission original data studies for linkable assets: agents can aggregate public data but cannot generate proprietary survey or client-pattern data
- Cross-client pattern recognition: the Scenius-style insights from real founder work are exactly what no competitor's AI can replicate

## 4. Visual identity (per post, varies)
- Images from the production sheets are produced by the visual-asset-agent (generated, diagrammed, or sourced with license and provenance). The human fills only slots marked human-only or held by the agent (provider failure, over cap, no licensable source), and can always upload their own to replace a candidate
- Original photography and video featuring real people, products, screens
- Evolving the visual style itself: the agents follow the brand system, they do not redesign it

## 5. Identity and relationships (link building's core, ongoing)
- Review, personalize, and send every outreach draft: add the one genuine detail about the recipient the agent left as a PERSONALIZE block
- Be the expert: quotes carry your name and credentials; podcast appearances are literally you talking
- Build and maintain journalist, podcast host, and community relationships over time
- Genuine community participation (Reddit, Slack groups, LinkedIn): agents mine the language, humans join the conversation

## 6. Approvals and business judgment (monthly, about an hour)
- Sign off the prune list: only a human knows a low-traffic page matters to a client, a partnership, or a sales motion
- Approve any spend above the cap, and any spend the technical monitor or plan proposes (CDN, migration, new tools)
- Quarterly strategy review: rerun the action plan when positioning, product, or market shifts, and decide when projections versus reality mean changing course

## 7. Legal, ethical, and reputational calls (as they arise)
- Claims compliance in regulated or sensitive topics
- Permission for client stories, testimonials, and data usage
- Final word on anything that could embarrass the brand: agents flag, humans decide

## The one-line division of labor
Agents do the volume, and with autopost on they ship it through guardrails; the specialist supplies ideas approval, truth, taste, identity, relationships, and sign-off on sends, prunes, and spend.
